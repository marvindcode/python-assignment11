1. Difference betweeen static and interactive visualizations
The static visualizations just shows a result like a picture, if is a graph shows the two axis and shows the information in the graph option we select, like bar, line, etc.
The interactive visualization shows the graphic with a dropdown menu showing the diferent option, this type of graph is more interactive, the displayed results
changes according the option selected.

2. Advantages of using dashboards for real time data
One of the advantages of updating the results every certain time, is that we can display the most recent results and not a result in the past.
For instance if the operations of a plant ends at 4:00pm the results can be displayed a few minutes after to see what results were at the end of the day 
and not results from previous day not evaluating what happen on the most recent date.

3. Dash and Plotly helps to display results in real time and interactive way which helps to get data insights that can be discussed on a team
and get a more deply analisis with information in real time.
